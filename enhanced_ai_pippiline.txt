### 📊 **Overall Structure Assessment**

Your `ai_solutions` directory is well-organized and comprehensive, but there are several areas for improvement:

### ✅ **Strengths:**

1. **Clear Module Separation**: Good separation between `core/`, `config/`, `models/`, `analytics/`, and `assets/`
2. **Comprehensive AI Pipeline**: Complete tracking system with person detection, age/gender classification, and analytics

4. **Performance Monitoring**: Built-in performance tracking and analytics generation

### ⚠️ **Issues and Recommendations:**

#### 1. **File Size and Organization Issues**

**Critical Issues:**
- `main_unified.py` (29KB, 732 lines) - **TOO LARGE** - violates single responsibility principle
- `websocket_manager.py` (13KB, 357 lines) - could be split
- `socketio_server.py` (11KB, 330 lines) - could be split

**Recommendations:**
```
ai_solutions/
```
